AgriShield

A smart irrigation and pest repellent system that waters crops only when they need it and keeps pests away automatically.

Role
Developer
Hardware

Control

The problem

Fixed irrigation schedules waste water on soil that is already wet, and pests can destroy a crop before a farmer notices. Both problems need constant attention that small farms can't always give.

What I built

  • Designed an IoT system on Arduino that reads field sensors and switches micro-irrigation on only when the soil needs water.
  • Wrote AI logic that runs on the device itself (at the edge), so decisions don't depend on an internet connection.
  • Added a pest repellent module and remote monitoring and control through the Blynk IoT app.
  • Published a peer-reviewed paper on the system's architecture and pest-repelling design, published in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library.

Results
