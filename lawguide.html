AI-LawGuide

A legal assistant that reads dense contracts, answers questions about them in plain language and matches people with the right lawyer.

The problem

Contracts are written for lawyers, not for the people signing them. Most people can't tell which clauses matter, and finding a lawyer with the right specialty nearby takes time.

What I built

  • Architected a retrieval-augmented (RAG) pipeline: PyMuPDF extracts text from contract PDFs, ChromaDB stores the chunks as embeddings, and the chatbot answers from the most relevant clauses.
  • Built a JWT-secured appointment booking API in FastAPI.
  • Wrote lawyer matching on three parameters: specialty, location and experience.
  • Connected a JavaScript front end to the chatbot and booking API.

Results

90%+comprehension accuracy on legal terminology
3parameters for lawyer matching
JWTsecured booking API
